Before you send
  • Remove OTPs, passwords, card numbers, UPI PINs, full account numbers, Aadhaar, and PAN.
  • Keep the suspicious text, link, claim, or payment instruction exactly as received.
  • Do not click, pay, install, share, or reply while you decide what to do.
What Kaval will review
  • Whether the claim has a source, date, location, original document, or named authority
  • Old content recirculated as new, screenshots without URLs, and emotional forwarding pressure
  • Whether the claim could cause financial, health, safety, or reputational harm
  • Which evidence would be needed before treating it as true

What this check looks for

  • Whether the claim has a source, date, location, original document, or named authority
  • Old content recirculated as new, screenshots without URLs, and emotional forwarding pressure
  • Whether the claim could cause financial, health, safety, or reputational harm
  • Which evidence would be needed before treating it as true

What the public page does not do

The public page does not impersonate sources, mass-query websites, or publish public claims about private people. It prepares a user-reviewed WhatsApp draft for the Kaval product flow.

  • It does not replace professional reporting or official emergency information.
  • It cannot verify private or local claims without sources.
  • It should not be used to harass or identify private people.

Safer next steps

  • Do not share high-stakes claims until you find the original source or credible reporting.
  • Check the date, location, source URL, and whether other reputable sources match.
  • Use Kaval for a deeper sourced check when the claim matters.

Questions people ask

Can AI fact-checking be wrong?

Yes. Good fact-checking depends on evidence, source quality, dates, and uncertainty. Treat this public tool as a triage step, not a final authority for high-stakes decisions.

What is the most useful thing to paste?

Paste the exact claim, the source if available, the date, and where you saw it. A vague summary is much harder to check reliably.
