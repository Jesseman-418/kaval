Before you send
  • Remove OTPs, passwords, card numbers, UPI PINs, full account numbers, Aadhaar, and PAN.
  • Keep the suspicious text, link, claim, or payment instruction exactly as received.
  • Do not click, pay, install, share, or reply while you decide what to do.

What the public page does not do

This public page does not fetch the target page, expand redirects, scan infrastructure, or probe hosts. It prepares a WhatsApp draft so Kaval can review the context in a controlled product flow.

  • It cannot prove a site is safe without opening or scanning it.
  • It does not expand short links in the public page.
  • A clean-looking URL can still host a phishing page later.

Safer next steps

  • Do not enter passwords, OTPs, card details, Aadhaar, PAN, or UPI PIN from a message link.
  • Open the official app or type the known official website yourself.
  • Forward the link to Kaval on WhatsApp if you need a deeper check with context.

Questions people ask

Can this checker tell me a link is definitely safe?

No public URL checker can promise that. This page opens a WhatsApp draft so Kaval can check the link and context without exposing an anonymous open scanner.

Why does Kaval avoid fetching URLs on this public page?

Open scanners can be abused to probe sites, trigger malicious pages, or enumerate infrastructure. Kaval keeps anonymous public pages lightweight and moves deeper checks into controlled product flows.
