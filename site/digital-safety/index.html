Kaval is an everyday assistant on WhatsApp. Send a message, link, claim, screenshot, or account concern and get a plain-English next step.

Can Kaval check a WhatsApp forward?

Yes. Forward the text, image, voice note, or link as you received it. Kaval can point out risk signals and help you decide what to do next.
