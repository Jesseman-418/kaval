Is your IndusInd Bank KYC SMS real or fake?

An unsolicited IndusInd Bank KYC link in a message is a warning sign. Check your status inside IndusMobile or by typing indusind.bank.in yourself.

An unsolicited KYC link is a strong warning sign, not a reason to click.

Three things that give it away.

  1. Look at who sent it

    A message from a personal number or WhatsApp account claiming to be your bank is a warning sign. Sender IDs can be copied, so verify through the official app or site rather than relying on the message.

  2. Check the web address, letter by letter

    Start at indusind.bank.in by typing it yourself. A lookalike such as indusind-kyc-verify.com is a warning sign, but the message link is not your source of truth.

  3. Notice the deadline

    "Today", "within 24 hours", and "account will be blocked" are pressure cues. Take a breath and check inside the app or official site before acting.

What IndusInd actually does, and what you got.

A real IndusInd KYC update

IndusInd Bank provides KYC through IndusMobile, its official site at indusind.bank.in (type it yourself), or a branch. Do not share an OTP, PIN, or password from a message link.

The message you got

IndusInd Bank: KYC verification pending. Account access will be suspended today. Update at [link] and share OTP.

Real name, real logo, borrowed panic. The link is the trap.

Clicked the link or shared an OTP?

It is fixable, and speed matters. Do these things now.

  • Do not enter anything more, and close the page.
  • From a phone you trust, change your net banking and UPI passwords.
  • Call IndusInd using the number on the back of your card, not any number from the message.
  • If money moved, call 1930 and report at cybercrime.gov.in the same day.

Questions people ask

Does IndusInd Bank send KYC links by SMS or WhatsApp?

Do not trust an unsolicited KYC link in a message. Verify your status inside IndusMobile, on indusind.bank.in (type it yourself), or at a branch. Do not share an OTP, PIN, or password from the message.

I already clicked the link or shared an OTP. What now?

Take a breath, it is fixable. From a phone you trust, change your net banking and UPI passwords, and call the bank using the number on the back of your card. If any money moved, call 1930 and file a report at cybercrime.gov.in the same day. The faster you report, the better the chance of a freeze or reversal.

How do I tell a real IndusInd message from a fake one?

A personal-number sender, an urgent deadline, or a link asking you to update KYC is a warning sign, not proof on its own. When in doubt, ignore the message and open IndusMobile yourself.

Why is the real IndusInd website indusind.bank.in and not a .com?

The .bank.in initiative is intended to make official banking domains easier to recognise, but a domain ending in .bank.in is not proof that a message is genuine. IndusInd Bank's listed address is indusind.bank.in; type it yourself or use the bank's app instead of following a message link.
