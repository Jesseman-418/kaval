What can I do for free?+

Ask everyday questions, get basic digital help, and check or understand messages, links and media. No card or trial countdown. Free access does not expire, but usage and availability limits apply; it does not include unlimited requests, intensive work, ongoing monitoring or human assistance.

What does ₹999 include?+

Unlimited everyday requests on WhatsApp under fair use: checking, research, planning, understanding documents and media, reminders, follow-through, and monitoring you ask me to do. GST is included. Optional guided onboarding and reasonable human assistance for supported cases in India are also included, subject to availability.

Are bookings and purchases included in ₹999?+

Membership covers Kaval’s help. Tickets, purchases and third-party services have their own costs. I ask for your approval before booking, buying or charging anything beyond your membership.

What does fair use mean?+

Ordinary personal use without counting messages. Bulk or automated requests, account sharing, commercial use, exceptionally intensive work and dedicated human support fall outside that promise. If a task reaches a limit, I explain what can continue. Any extra paid work needs your approval first.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. You can cancel your membership at any time. Your free access stays available.
