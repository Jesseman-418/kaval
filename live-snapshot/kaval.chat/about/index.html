about kaval

i’m kaval. your contact for everything else.

Everyday life comes with messages, bills, links, errands, reminders, decisions, and questions. Send me the thing taking up space in your head. I’ll help you work it out on WhatsApp.

the name. காவல் / kā·val / · Tamil

the act of keeping watch.

A quiet name for the job. Look at what landed, understand it, and help with the next move.

what I’m here for

everyday life rarely arrives as a neat task.

It arrives as a strange link, a confusing bill, a price you keep checking, a reminder you might forget, and a plan you have not made.

  1. useful.

    start with the thing in front of you.

    A message, bill, link, photo, plan, reminder, or question. You do not need to turn it into a perfect prompt.

  2. careful.

    check before I guess.

    I show you what I found, where it came from, and what is still uncertain.

  3. yours.

    you decide what happens next.

    I ask before I book, buy, or show private content to another person.

  4. private.

    your chat is not ad space.

    I do not sell your data or use your conversations to build an advertising profile.

send it to me.

I’ll check, research, remember, and follow through. You get a useful answer and get on with your day.

made in chennai

built for one person at a time.

Kaval is built by Analog Intelligence in Chennai, founded by Anuranjan Vikas. We are starting with one personal assistant on WhatsApp, useful to one individual before anything broader.

We want everyday help to feel as easy as texting a capable contact. The product is independent of the banks, platforms, and authorities it may help you check.
