WhatsApp Scam Checker

Most WhatsApp scams start with a familiar-looking message and one rushed request: click, pay, install, or share a code. Send it to Kaval and get a plain answer before you act.

Keep the original wording, link, or image. Remove private codes and account details before you send it.

what to look for.

  • Fake KYC warnings from banks, wallets, telecom providers, and delivery companies
  • OTP, UPI PIN, QR code, and refund-payment traps
  • Job, investment, lottery, and prize messages that ask for a fee first
  • Short links, lookalike domains, and WhatsApp-only support numbers
  • Forwarded misinformation, old screenshots, and copied emergency claims

useful context.

Built for forwards, screenshots, links, and voice notes

A risky WhatsApp message is rarely just text. It may be a shortened link, a fake bank screenshot, a copied news claim, or a voice note telling everyone to act quickly. Kaval reads the item in context instead of treating every forward the same way.

Useful for an uncertain moment

The safest response is often a simple pause. Forward the message, get the risk pattern, and decide on the next move before replying.

Focused on action, not just labels

A scam assessment is only useful when it tells you what to do next. The answer separates "do not click," "verify in the official app," "call your bank," "report this," and "safe to ignore."

questions people ask.

Can Kaval check a WhatsApp forward without me opening the link?

Yes. You can paste or forward the message and URL. Kaval can inspect the link and surrounding message signals before you click it.

What should I do if a WhatsApp message asks for OTP or UPI PIN?

Treat it as dangerous. Do not share OTPs or UPI PINs with anyone. Use the official bank, wallet, or app support channel if you need to verify the issue.

Can I use Kaval for a message someone sent me?

Yes. Paste or forward the message, call script, screenshot, or payment request you are unsure about, then review the suggested next step.
