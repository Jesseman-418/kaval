questions people ask.

Does a breach mean my account is hacked right now?

Not always. It means some data may be exposed. The right response is to secure the affected account and any account that reused the same password.

What should I secure first after an email breach?

Secure the email account itself first, then banking, wallets, social accounts, work apps, and any account using the same password.

Why does a leaked phone number matter?

A leaked phone number can lead to spam, OTP phishing, WhatsApp impersonation, SIM-swap attempts, and targeted payment scams.
